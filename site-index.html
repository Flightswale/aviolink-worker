Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create a new email ID?

No. You connect your existing Gmail / Google Workspace inbox with one click ("Sign in with Google", read-only). Alerts are delivered to the same inbox and on WhatsApp. Non-Gmail agencies get a private @aviolink.in forwarding address instead.

Will you see my personal or client emails?

Our system reads only airline, booking and vendor mails. Everything else is discarded the moment it arrives — never stored, never read by a human. You can connect a dedicated booking mailbox only (like ops@ or ticketing@) if you prefer, and disconnect anytime from your Google account.

Which airlines and vendors are supported?

All of them. Our AI reads any airline, GDS, consolidator or B2B portal format — Air India, IndiGo, Emirates, Qatar, Cleartrip B2B, TBO, TripJack and anything else, in any language. No lists to maintain.

What does it cost after the trial?

Plans start at ₹1,999/month — less than one goodwill refund for a single missed flight. No setup fee, cancel anytime. Your 30-day trial is full-featured and needs no card.

Do my clients get the alerts directly?

Today you receive the formatted, WhatsApp-ready message and forward it in one tap — you stay in control. Direct-to-client WhatsApp delivery under your agency's name is rolling out to trial agencies shortly.

Is any software installed on my computers?

Nothing. Aviolink runs fully in the cloud. Your computers, your GDS and your passwords are never touched.
