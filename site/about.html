About Aviolink

Aviation software built from the ticketing desk.

We are travel professionals first. Aviolink exists because we lived the problem — missed schedule-change mails, stranded passengers and the calls that follow.

Our story

From a busy agency inbox to a product.

Aviolink started inside a working Pune travel agency. Every week, airline schedule changes and cancellations arrived by email among hundreds of other mails — and every week someone had to find them before a client reached the airport.

We built a system that reads those mails the way an experienced ticketing executive would — knowing an airline PNR from a vendor reference, a protected rebooking from a voluntary change, and a GDS status code from noise. Today that system is FlightAlert.
