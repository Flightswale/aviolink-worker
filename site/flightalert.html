Under the hood

How FlightAlert works

1

Detect

Every new mail is checked against worldwide airline, GDS and vendor patterns. Promotions, OTPs, newsletters and your own outgoing requests are skipped.

2

Understand

AI extracts PNR, passengers, airline, every leg and the old vs new timings — from mail text and PDF attachments, in any format or language.

3

Match & deliver

The change is matched to the original ticket for names and contact number, checked for duplicates, and sent for your one-tap approval — then to your client on WhatsApp.

FAQ

Questions agencies ask

Do I need a new email ID?

No. You connect the inbox you already use with "Sign in with Google". If you are not on Gmail / Google Workspace, you get a private forwarding address instead.

Will you read my personal or client emails?

Only airline, booking and vendor mails are processed. Everything else is skipped automatically and its content is never stored or read by a person. Access is read-only and you can disconnect anytime.

Do my clients get alerts directly?

Yes, after you approve. Each alert is shown to you first; one tap sends it to your passenger on WhatsApp with your agency name and number.

What about delays?

Delay and revised-timing notices are treated like schedule changes. If the flight is within 48 hours the alert is marked URGENT and delivered immediately.

Is anything installed on my computers or GDS?

Nothing. Aviolink runs fully in the cloud. Your GDS, computers and passwords are never touched.

What if a mail is missed or parsed wrongly?

Every mail we process is logged. Tell us the PNR and we re-check it within minutes — and improve the system so it does not happen again.
