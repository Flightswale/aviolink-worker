Data & security

Read-only. Minimal. In India.

Connecting a mailbox is a serious act of trust. Here is exactly what Aviolink can and cannot do.

👁️

Read-only access

We request Google's read-only Gmail permission. Aviolink cannot send, delete, move or modify any mail.

🔑

No passwords

Sign-in happens on Google's own page. We never see or store your password and you can revoke access anytime.

✂️

Minimal storage

Only flight details from airline mails are stored — PNR, passengers, legs, timings. Other mails are skipped and not stored.

🗄️

Encrypted, in India

Data is kept in an encrypted managed database in the Mumbai region, with access limited to the service itself.

🚫

Never sold, never shared

Your data is used only to deliver alerts to you and, after your approval, to your own passengers.

🧾

Google API policy

Our use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

Disconnect anytime at myaccount.google.com/connections · Request deletion at support@aviolink.in · Full details in our Privacy policy.
