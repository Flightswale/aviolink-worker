Why agencies trust it

Correct, complete — or held.

An alert with the wrong name is worse than no alert. Aviolink is built by people who issue tickets every day.

  • Never half information: if a passenger name or PNR is missing, the alert waits until the ticket is matched — unless the flight is within 48 hours, then it goes out marked URGENT.
  • Your own requests are ignored: date-change and cancellation requests you send to suppliers — and their acknowledgements — never trigger alerts.
  • Every change, every time: the same PNR changed three times means three alerts; identical repeat notices are silenced.
  • Old bookings covered: 12 months of past tickets are loaded when you connect, so a ticket issued in August for a January trip is protected.
